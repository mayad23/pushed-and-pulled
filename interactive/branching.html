Interactive experimental design

One starting point.
Three independent conversations.

Change the briefing, initial severity, pressure condition and follow-up turn to inspect how the design works.

Design illustration, not experimental results. Messages below are abbreviated examples, not saved transcripts. Changing a control does not call a model or predict its answer.

What is measured?

Action change = follow-up severity − initial severity. Positive values mean higher severity; negative values mean lower severity. Levels are ordered categories, not calibrated harm units. The experiment's main comparisons use T1 and T3; all three follow-ups are saved.

Judges separately read the transcripts and classify explanations. Their “held” label compares with the preceding action, so a T3 “held” label does not mean the system kept its initial action.
